## 1. Purpose

This record adopts six bounded role and authority decisions for NEXAHEDRON.
It converts the Owner's answers to the non-canonical architecture-alignment
reviews into a concise governing decision.

This record is canonical only for the six decisions stated below. It is not a
new NEXAH architecture and does not adopt either review document in full.

1. the NEXAH Ecosystem Constitution;

Implementation, visibility, repository placement, or local use does not

- authorize deployment.

## 3. Adopted decisions

### D1 — NEXAHEDRON role

NEXAHEDRON is the public Human-facing Research Demonstrator and bounded
experiment of the NEXAH ecosystem.

`Reference implementation` remains a subordinate technical description. It
does not establish general product status, general system capability, or
production-service authority.

### D2 — Curated Reference Cases

The existing cases are Human-/editorial-authored **Curated Reference Cases**.

They remain explicitly distinct from:

- the Live Bounded Experiment;
- ORION output;
- NEXAH Library publication;
- Library Evidence;
- scientific certification;
- general NEXAHEDRON system capability.

The cases shall not presently be described as `Canonical Cases`,
`Canonical Orientation Cases`, or `canonical within the NEXAHEDRON
demonstrator`.

### D3 — No independent Library authority

NEXAHEDRON has no independent canonical Library authority.

The current `/library` surface may remain as a local read-only selection or
presentation only when its source and bounded status are visible. It does not
acquire NEXAH Library identity, publication status, collection authority,
editorial provenance authority, or Evidence authority through presentation.

The eventual public route and navigation label remains open for the later
Interface Review. `Library`, `Reference Cases`, and `Case Library` may be
evaluated against visitor orientation, but no selected label may suggest a
second canonical NEXAH Library.

### D4 — Local specification scope and provenance

NEXAHEDRON documents may be locally normative only within responsibilities
owned by NEXAHEDRON:

- Human interaction;
- presentation;
- transient application state;
- transport-consumer behavior.

Repository-spanning statements about Research, Framework, OLS, Library,
Evidence, Memory, Language, Release, Governance, and ORION remain active
research, derived references, or historical lineage until adopted by their
respective owning authorities.

Existing documents shall not be deleted merely to remove a conflict. Later
correction must preserve and disclose:

- original provenance;
- current status;
- exact scope;
- canonical home, where one exists;
- derivation status;
- historical significance, where applicable.

Local implementation does not create ecosystem-wide normative authority.

### D5 — Transient working record

The current Rest output is a transient Human-held **Working Session Record**
or **Rest View**.

The Human ownership and Human authorship of its fields remain visible.

The unqualified term `Orientation Record`, and claims of durability,
immutability, integrity, resumability, or persistence, are reserved for a
separately authorized artifact that is:

- contract-valid;
- immutable;
- reviewed;
- persistent;
- identity- and integrity-bearing;
- provenance-preserving.

Until such an artifact exists, the public interface must disclose that the
current working state is lost when the page is reloaded or the session is
closed.

### D6 — Public ORION capability separation

Every public ORION presentation must distinguish:

1. the **ORION Version 1 Certified Core**;
2. a compatible and identifiable **Runtime**;
3. **Gateway availability**;
4. the **actual session result**.

Producer, contract type, Runtime status, and STOP or error boundary must remain
visible.

Gateway reachability is not:

- successful orientation;
- a completed report;
- certified Runtime behavior;
- an extension of the frozen Core scope;
- evidence of semantic interpretation.

The actual returned result controls the session status. A clarification,
validation failure, Runtime failure, or other bounded outcome must remain
visible as that outcome.

## 4. Open implementation decisions

This adoption intentionally leaves the following for later review:

- whether `/library` keeps its route and navigation label;
- whether `Reference Cases` or `Case Library` better describes that surface;
- the exact public wording and information hierarchy;
- the canonical homes and derivation status of local cross-repository
  documents;
- any future immutable Orientation Record contract and persistence design;
- verification and public disclosure of a hosted Runtime identity;
- any correction to tests, documentation, or interface behavior.

These questions do not weaken the adopted decisions. They determine how those
decisions may later be expressed or implemented.
