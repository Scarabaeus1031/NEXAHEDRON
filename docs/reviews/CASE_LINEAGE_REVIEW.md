# Canonical Case Lineage Review

- Scope: editorial provenance for the five current canonical Orientation Cases
- Status: implemented review record
- Case content, scientific claims, Evidence, and canonical twelve-part structure: unchanged

## Selection rule

Case Lineage records how an orientation developed. It is selected only from
traceable Library identities, documented editorial reviews, canonical examples,
and repository history. It does not establish the truth of a Case's claims.

Evidence remains a separate record of what supports those claims. Library Works
named below contributed visual language, relationships, or editorial structure;
they are not silently presented as scientific evidence. Catalogued exploratory
Works retain that status even when their useful structures are carried forward.

## Plate Tectonics

1. **Canonical demonstration:** `examples/orientation/README.md`, *A First
   Orientation — Plate Tectonics*, established the bounded intention, evidence
   map, uncertainty, boundaries, and possible continuations.
2. **Evidence boundary:** the USGS, NOAA, and NSF GAGE sources shaped and
   support the subject orientation. They remain external Evidence and are not
   described as NEXAH Library provenance.
3. **Editorial transformation:**
   `docs/reviews/PUBLIC_WEBSITE_EXPERIENCE_REVIEW.md` records the 2026 movement
   from the demonstration into the public Case hierarchy and its later museum
   composition.
4. **Canonicalization:** the public `Plate Tectonics` Case is labeled
   `Canonical Case v1.0`.

No NEXAH Library Work is inserted into this lineage because the documented
origin is the canonical demonstration and its public Evidence, not the
Foundational Collection's Library-first curation.

## What is a Map?

1. **Library source material:** MAP ATLAS, NEXAH ATLAS — A Cartography of
   Perspectives, THE LANGUAGE ATLAS, and THE CARTOGRAPHY LABORATORY.
2. **Developed structure:** *The Map of Maps* and *A Lens, Not a Final Theory*
   were distilled into territory → purpose → selection → symbol and scale →
   map.
3. **Editorial review:** the Foundational Collection review records the choice
   to begin with a geographic definition and bound the extension to conceptual
   maps.
4. **Canonicalization:** `What is a Map?`, `Canonical Case v1.0`.

## What is Scale?

1. **Library source material:** THE ATLAS OF ATLASES, NEXAH — Maps of Human
   Reality, NEXAH ATLAS — A Cartography of Perspectives, and FIELD ATLAS I —
   WATER.
2. **Developed structure:** human-to-microscopic transitions and the 4 + 1 view
   were distilled into detail → local pattern → system → region → planet.
3. **Editorial review:** the Foundational Collection review records the
   rejection of resemblance as proof of a shared cross-scale mechanism.
4. **Canonicalization:** `What is Scale?`, `Canonical Case v1.0`.

## What is a Scientific Model?

1. **Library source material:** ORIENTATION SCIENCE, THE CARTOGRAPHY
   LABORATORY, NEXAH ATLAS — A Cartography of Perspectives, and THE LANGUAGE
   BOOK; the local `MODEL_RELATIONSHIP_MAP.md` review constrained their
   relationship.
2. **Developed structure:** *From Models to Maps* and the Validation Laboratory
   were distilled into phenomenon → question → representation → output →
   comparison.
3. **Editorial review:** the Foundational Collection review records the removal
   of a false models-versus-maps choice and the separate grounding of model
   validation in external Evidence.
4. **Canonicalization:** `What is a Scientific Model?`, `Canonical Case v1.0`.
