# Code of Conduct

NEXAHEDRON follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our commitment

We commit to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected conduct

- Be respectful, specific and evidence-aware.
- Critique work and claims rather than people.
- Preserve uncertainty and disagreement honestly.
- Respect privacy, provenance, licensing and Human authority.
- Accept correction and take responsibility for harmful conduct.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, publication of private information and sustained disruption are
not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to
[contact@nexah.de](mailto:contact@nexah.de) with the subject
`NEXAHEDRON Code of Conduct`. Reports will be handled as confidentially as
reasonably possible. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation or
take other proportionate action. Decisions should document the conduct
addressed without unnecessarily exposing affected people.

This policy applies in project repositories, issue trackers, reviews, public
events and other spaces where someone represents NEXAHEDRON.
